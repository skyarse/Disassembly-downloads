### Added

- Verified transfers to destinations on other filesystems, with a local history of failures and recovery after interruption.
- Season folders based on episode names and grouped transfers for videos and matching subtitles.
- Content-based duplicate lookup and confirmed moves for game folders and multi-file installers.
- Exact destination previews, including collision suffixes, and undo for grouped moves and batches.

### Fixed

- Compound extensions such as `tar.gz` and `tar.xz` now work in rule previews, automatic sorting, and exclusions.

The release includes the x86_64 AppImage, its update index, SHA-256 checksums, the source commit, the Cargo lockfile, and packaging tool provenance.
